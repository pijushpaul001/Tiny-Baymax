A few things to know

  • Your messages are sent to the Baymax server and may be sent to Google’s Gemini service to generate replies.
  • The server can store redacted conversation history, ratings, settings, and approved memories. Automatic memory is on by default; send /memory-off to disable it and /memory-on to re-enable it. You can inspect, export, and delete stored data.
  • Baymax is a supportive AI prototype, not a doctor, medical device, or emergency service. Do not use it for emergencies.
